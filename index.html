How it works

From timeline to dashboard.

  1. Install the extension

    Choose your browser below and add PostsDash from its extension store.

  2. Open X and sign in

    Use your existing X account on x.com to access your Following feed.

  3. Launch PostsDash

    Select PostsDash in the X sidebar to open your dashboard. You can enable automatic launch in PostsDash settings.

Before you install

Questions about PostsDash.

Is PostsDash free?

Yes. PostsDash is a free browser extension.

Which browsers does it support?

PostsDash is available for Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Opera. Choose your browser in the install section below.

Do I need an X account?

Yes. PostsDash works with your signed-in X session to display posts from your Following feed.

Does it work inside the X mobile app?

PostsDash runs on x.com in a browser with the extension installed. It does not change the X mobile app.
